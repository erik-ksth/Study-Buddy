# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Study Buddy is designed primarily for students who want a calmer, more focused environment for studying. People doing other kinds of focused work may still benefit, but student needs and study workflows take priority.

## Product Purpose

Study Buddy helps students focus without distraction by bringing the tools they need for a study session—timing, tasks, music, notes, flashcards, and progress—into one cohesive workspace. Success means students can begin and sustain focused work without needing to move between distracting apps.

## Positioning

A focused study workspace that makes useful productivity tools feel inviting, cohesive, and distraction-free.

## Brand Personality

Attractive, useful, and focused. The experience should feel aesthetically distinctive and enjoyable without allowing decoration to compete with the work a student is trying to complete.

## Anti-references

Study Buddy should never feel cluttered, distracting, awkward to use, or visually generic. Avoid generic AI-generated interface patterns and novelty that weakens usability.

## Design Principles

1. Protect focus: every feature and interaction should reduce context switching and unnecessary attention demands.
2. Make usefulness inviting: practical study tools should feel approachable and satisfying without becoming decorative toys.
3. Earn visual distinction: preserve Study Buddy's themed identity and use deliberate, recognizable details instead of generic interface trends.
4. Keep workflows smooth: actions should be predictable, responsive, and require as little friction as the task allows.
5. Add capability without clutter: new features should use progressive disclosure and preserve a calm primary workspace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional accessibility requirements have been specified. New work should maintain clear keyboard interaction, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion alternatives so the focus experience remains usable across common access needs.
